How MediFind Works

Designed for ease of use by families, elders, and rural community members.

1

Search

Enter the exact medicine name or generic salt written on your prescription, along with your village or town name.

2

Compare

Review reported in-stock status and listed prices across multiple nearby participating medical stores.

3

Confirm

Contact the pharmacy to verify real-time stock and hold your medicine before travelling, saving time and bus fares.

Your Health Matters

Please read these essential safety guidelines before using medicine information.

Simulated Offline Data Pharmacy stock and prices are provided for demonstration purposes in this offline prototype. No real commercial inventory connection exists.
Stock Can Change Rapidly Availability reported by participating pharmacies can change before you arrive. Always call to confirm availability before visiting.
Check Formulation & Strength Always verify the medicine brand name, strength (e.g. 500 mg), dosage form, and pack size against your official medical prescription.
Consult Medical Professionals Do not change prescribed treatment based solely on price. Always consult a qualified doctor or licensed pharmacist for medical advice.
